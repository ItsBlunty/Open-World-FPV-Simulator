# Sound sources

Every recording here is CC0 (public domain dedication). Freesound files were taken from their 128 kbps MP3 previews,
cut and re-encoded to Opus (2026-10-19). Everything else the game plays is synthesised in `apps/fly/src/audio/`.

| File | What | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `strike.ogg` | an FPV quad's props hitting a window frame (4.12-4.75 s) | https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/854351/ (from a US Marine Corps video, public domain) | qubodup | CC0 |
| `whistle.ogg` | a steam whistle blast (16.95-20.9 s) | https://freesound.org/people/CHallSmith/sounds/870853/ | CHallSmith | CC0 |
| `standing.ogg` | a steam engine standing at a station, blowing off (0-16 s, looped) | https://freesound.org/people/cauby43/sounds/473904/ | cauby43 | CC0 |
| `park.ogg` | a spring city park, birds (50-112 s, looped) | https://freesound.org/people/Garuda1982/sounds/730227/ | Garuda1982 | CC0 |
| `city.ogg` | a Toronto street, traffic and people (0-92 s, looped) | https://freesound.org/people/Oldome/sounds/856315/ | Oldome | CC0 |
| `hit-hard-00N.ogg` | impactGeneric_light_00N | Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | Kenney | CC0 |
| `hit-soft-00N.ogg` | impactSoft_medium_00N | Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 |
